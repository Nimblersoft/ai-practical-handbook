# Editorial glossary

These are terms for maintaining the handbook. For AI vocabulary and practical guides, start with the [reader indexes](README.md).

**Concept**: The idea being explained, regardless of which language or term a reader uses.

_Avoid_: translation, when referring to the idea itself.

**Entry**: The explanation of one concept across all available languages.

_Avoid_: edition, when referring to the concept across languages.

**Workflow**: A reader-facing guide for completing a task and checking its result.

_Avoid_: entry, which explains a concept rather than walking through a task.

**Edition**: An entry or workflow as a reader sees it in one language.

_Avoid_: separate entry, which suggests a different concept.

**Alias**: Another term or acronym a reader may encounter for the same concept.

_Avoid_: related term, which names a distinct concept.

**Index**: The list in which a reader looks up terms and aliases.

_Avoid_: glossary, when referring only to navigation.
