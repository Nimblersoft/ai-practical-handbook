# AI assistant

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**AI assistant.** A tool that uses AI to help a person with tasks such as drafting, summarizing, or finding information. It may work through chat, voice, or features built into another app.

**Example:** A writing assistant drafts a reply to a customer. You check the details, edit the wording, and send the message yourself.

**See also:** [Chatbot](../chatbot/en.md), [AI agent](../ai-agent/en.md).
