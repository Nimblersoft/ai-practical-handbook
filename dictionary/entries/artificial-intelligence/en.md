# Artificial intelligence (AI)

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Artificial intelligence (AI).** A broad term for computer systems that draw on patterns or knowledge to make predictions, suggest options, make decisions, or create content. Generating text is one use of AI, not the whole field.

**Example:** An email service can use AI to spot likely spam without generating any text.

**See also:** [Machine learning](../machine-learning/en.md), [generative AI](../generative-ai/en.md).
