# Bias

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Bias.** A systematic slant in a system’s results. It can come from the examples it was trained on, the way it was designed, or the way people use it. In practice, it can mean some groups are treated less fairly than others.

**Example:** A voice tool that works well for one accent but regularly mishears another may reflect bias. Testing it with speakers from different regions helps reveal that gap.

**See also:** [Evaluation](../evaluation/en.md), [training data](../training-data/en.md).
