# Chatbot

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Chatbot.** A program you interact with through a conversation, usually by text. Some chatbots offer preset choices and replies; others use AI to generate their answers.

**Example:** A delivery chatbot can ask for an order number and reply with its status. Another chatbot might also handle a question you phrase in your own words.

**See also:** [AI assistant](../ai-assistant/en.md), [generative AI](../generative-ai/en.md).
