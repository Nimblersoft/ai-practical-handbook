# Context window

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Context window.** The limit on how much information a model can work with at once. A chat app may show the whole conversation even when it sends only recent parts or a summary to the model.

**Example:** In a long conversation about an event, a chatbot may miss a budget you mentioned at the start. Restating the amount makes it available for the next reply.

**See also:** [Token](../token/en.md), [memory](../memory/en.md).
