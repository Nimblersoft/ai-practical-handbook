# Deepfake

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Deepfake.** AI-generated or AI-altered media that can make a person appear to say or do something they did not. It can be a photo, a video, or an audio recording. A deepfake can be created with consent, but it can also be used to deceive.

**Example:** A voice note sounds like a family member asking for emergency money. Call the number you already know rather than trusting the voice.
