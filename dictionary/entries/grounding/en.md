# Grounding

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Grounding.** Giving an AI system reference material, such as a document, to base its answer on. The system can still misread the source or make claims it does not support, so check the cited passage.

**Example:** Give an assistant the company’s travel policy and ask it to quote the rule about meal expenses. Compare the quote with the policy before submitting a claim.

**See also:** [Prompt](../prompt/en.md), [hallucination](../hallucination/en.md).
