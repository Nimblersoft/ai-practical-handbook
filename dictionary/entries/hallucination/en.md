# Hallucination

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Hallucination.** When an AI system presents false or unsupported information as if it were true. A confident tone or a realistic-looking citation is not proof.

**Example:** A chatbot gives you the title and author of a study that does not exist. Check the original publisher or a library catalog before citing it.

**See also:** [Grounding](../grounding/en.md), [human oversight](../human-oversight/en.md).
