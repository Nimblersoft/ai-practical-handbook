# Human oversight

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Human oversight.** People monitor an AI system’s use or results and have the authority to intervene, correct a mistake, or stop an action. *Human in the loop* often refers to a specific setup in which a person reviews or approves a step.

**Example:** An assistant drafts a public notice. An editor checks the opening hours and approves the notice before it goes out.
