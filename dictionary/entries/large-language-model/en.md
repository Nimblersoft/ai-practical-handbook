# Large language model (LLM)

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Large language model (LLM).** An AI model trained on large collections of material to work with language. Many generate text by repeatedly predicting what could come next. Producing convincing prose is different from checking facts.

**Example:** An LLM can turn rough notes into a clear agenda. It can also add a meeting time that was never in your notes.
