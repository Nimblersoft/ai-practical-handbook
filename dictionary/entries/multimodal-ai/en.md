# Multimodal AI

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Multimodal AI.** AI that can work with more than one kind of information, such as text, images, or sound. Which kinds it can accept or produce depends on the tool.

**Example:** You show an assistant a photo of a paper menu and ask it in writing what dishes are marked vegetarian. Check the menu yourself if an allergy or dietary restriction matters.
