# Output

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Output.** What an AI tool produces: for example, a reply, an image, a prediction, or a recommendation. An output is a result, not a guarantee that the result is right or ready to use.

**Example:** You request a two-sentence summary of a notice. The summary is the output; compare it with the notice before forwarding it.

**See also:** [Prompt](../prompt/en.md), [hallucination](../hallucination/en.md).
