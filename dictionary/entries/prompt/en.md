# Prompt

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Prompt.** The request or instruction you give an AI tool to guide its response. It can include a question, relevant information, and the format you want back.

**Example:** Instead of “Summarize this,” try “Summarize this school notice in three bullets. Include what parents need to bring and when.”

**See also:** [Output](../output/en.md), [context window](../context-window/en.md).
