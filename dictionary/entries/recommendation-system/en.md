# Recommendation system (recommender system)

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Recommendation system (recommender system).** A system that selects or ranks things to show you, such as videos, products, or songs. It may use your activity, popular choices, or similarities between items.

**Example:** After you listen to a few songs by one artist, a music app suggests other artists. Those suggestions can reflect what the app is designed to promote, not just your tastes.

**See also:** [Artificial intelligence](../artificial-intelligence/en.md), [bias](../bias/en.md).
