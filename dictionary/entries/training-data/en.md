# Training data

[English index](../../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Training data.** The information used while developing a model so it can learn patterns. This differs from the information you provide for one response. Whether a provider later uses what you submit as training data depends on its policies and your settings.

**Example:** A model for recognizing street signs might learn from thousands of labeled photos. A photo you submit later is an input to that tool, not automatically part of its training data.

**See also:** [Machine learning](../machine-learning/en.md), [personal data](../personal-data/en.md).
