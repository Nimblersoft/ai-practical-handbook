# Sources and further reading

[English index](../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

- [OECD: Definition of an AI system](https://doi.org/10.1787/623da898-en) — the broad definition of AI and the difference between predictions, recommendations, and decisions.
- [NIST: AI Risk Management Framework](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) — reliability, fairness, privacy, and oversight.
- [NIST: Generative AI Profile](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) — what can go wrong with generative AI, including confident falsehoods (called *confabulations* in the report).
