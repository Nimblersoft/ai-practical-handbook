# Check an AI answer against a source

[Workflow index](../en.md) · [Español](es.md)

**Goal:** Decide which parts of an AI-generated announcement you can share. You need a chat assistant and the original notice; no connector is required.

1. Paste this practice notice: **“The library workshop starts at 10. Entry is free. Bring your own laptop.”** Label it *Source*. Then paste this draft separately: **“Come at 10 for a free workshop. Laptops and lunch are provided.”**
2. Ask: **“Break the draft into individual claims. For each, quote the exact words in the source that support or contradict it. If the source says nothing about a claim, mark it ‘not stated’.”**
3. Compare the quotes with the original. Cross out contradicted claims and set unsupported ones aside. Ask for a new draft using only what you verified.

**Check the result:** The time and free entry are supported. Providing laptops contradicts “bring your own laptop.” Lunch is **not stated**; that does not mean lunch is unavailable. A usable draft is: **“Free library workshop at 10. Bring your own laptop.”**

**If the assistant invents a quote:** Search the original for its exact words. If they aren't there, remove that claim or ask the organizer directly before sharing it.
